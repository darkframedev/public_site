Deep in the heart of a decaying Midwest town, drowning in oppressive fog and the sickly glow of old sodium vapor streetlights, secrets don't stay buried—they just get harder to read.

Dead Letters is a harrowing 1990s analog horror investigation where you piece together the final, frantic communications of people who simply disappeared. You are navigating a gritty, corrupted paper trail—photocopied municipal documents, heavily redacted police files, and blood-stained personal correspondence—the "dead letters" someone tried desperately to destroy.

The warning is clear: Some things should be left unread. By the time you find out why, it might be too late to look away.
